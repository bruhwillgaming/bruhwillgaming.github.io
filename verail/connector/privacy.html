Connector Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Connector collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the Discord bot. Please read it carefully so you understand what information the bot processes.

1. Operator Information

Connector is operated by Matthew Sweskie.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact the operator using the contact details provided below.

2. Information We Collect

Connector may process the following information when you use its verification and account-linking features:

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • Your Discord server ID.
  • Your connected platform usernames or account identifiers.
  • Verification status and related verification information.
  • Information required to process privacy requests.

Connector does not intentionally collect passwords for your external social media accounts.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Connector is used to:

  • Verify ownership of supported external accounts.
  • Connect Discord accounts with external platform accounts.
  • Store and maintain verification records.
  • Provide verification-related bot features.
  • Process privacy requests and permanent opt-outs.
  • Prevent repeated verification where a permanent opt-out applies.

4. External Services

Connector communicates with external services to perform account verification. These services may receive information necessary to complete the requested verification.

  • Discord API
  • YouTube Data API
  • Roblox Users API
  • X API
  • Twitch API

Your use of these external platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Instagram and TikTok verification are not currently implemented as supported verification methods.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Connector stores certain information in its local data files to maintain verification functionality.

Pending verification records: These normally expire after 15 minutes, unless a different expiry period has been configured.

Completed verification records: These are retained until deleted through the available privacy controls or removed by the operator. They do not currently have an automatic expiry period.

Permanent opt-out records: A Discord user ID may be retained in order to enforce a permanent opt-out and prevent future storage of verification information.

6. Data Sharing

Connector does not sell personal information.

Information may be sent to the external services listed above when necessary to perform verification requests. Connector may also process information within Discord to provide its features.

Information is not intentionally shared for advertising purposes.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Connector provides privacy controls that may allow you to view, export, or request deletion of information stored by the bot.

You may also request a permanent opt-out. A permanent opt-out prevents the bot from storing future verification information associated with your Discord user ID.

Some information, such as a Discord user ID used to enforce a permanent opt-out, may need to remain stored for that restriction to work.

Deleting information from Connector does not necessarily delete information held by Discord or external platforms. It also does not necessarily remove Discord server roles, independent server logs, or backups.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by Connector. However, no storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive account credentials with the bot or its operator.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Connector's features, data handling practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published at this page. The date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about Connector's handling of information, contact the operator:

Matthew Sweskie connector.gpdr@proton.me
