Privacy Policy

What information Railu may handle and how it is used.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Railu collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you interact with the Bot. Information processed depends on the features enabled and how the Bot is configured.

2. Information We May Process

Discord account and server information

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Channel IDs.
  • Relevant role and permission information.
  • Server configuration and preferences.

Moderation information

  • Warning records and moderation case history.
  • User IDs associated with moderation actions.
  • Reasons entered by moderators.
  • Timeout, kick, and ban records.

Security information

  • Spam and repeated-message detection events.
  • Suspicious activity and anti-raid alerts.
  • Relevant audit-log events.
  • Discord AutoMod execution information.

Reports and configuration

  • Reports submitted through supported features.
  • Allowlist entries and server settings.
  • Privacy-related requests.
Some features inspect message content to detect spam or repeated messages. Whether message content is stored or logged depends on the deployed version and configuration. Verify the actual code before making a definitive statement about message-content storage.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide moderation and security features, detect spam and suspicious activity, maintain moderation records, send alerts to configured channels, apply server settings, respond to privacy requests, and diagnose errors.

4. Storage and Security

Railu uses a local SQLite database configured with database encryption. Encryption is intended to help protect stored information, but no database, computer, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

The Bot operator is responsible for protecting the computer running Railu, database files, encryption keys, and backups.

5. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be transmitted through Discord when necessary for Bot functionality, including moderation actions, alerts, and command responses.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, communities, the Bot, or its operator.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as it is reasonably needed for moderation, security, and operational purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the data type and the Bot’s configuration.

Before publishing: Replace this with the actual retention periods used by your deployed version of Railu. Include moderation records, warnings, security events, reports, and backups.

7. Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your Discord account. Submit requests using the contact information below. Include your Discord user ID and enough information to identify relevant records. Never send your password or account credentials.

Some records may need to be retained for legitimate moderation, security, legal, or operational reasons.

8. Children’s Privacy

Railu is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact the operator.

9. Third-Party Services

Railu uses Discord’s platform and API. Discord may independently process information under its own policies. See the Discord Privacy Policy.

10. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Railu’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
