Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Verifier, a Discord verification bot operated by Verail ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the bot.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Verifier, we may collect and store:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server (guild) IDs.
  • Verification status and verification timestamps.
  • Verification attempt counts.
  • Temporary verification session information.
  • Hashed verification codes required to validate sessions.
  • Server verification settings and configuration.
  • Privacy preferences, including opt-out settings.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected by Verifier is used to:

  • Provide Discord server verification functionality.
  • Confirm whether a user has completed verification.
  • Manage verification attempts and temporary sessions.
  • Apply server-specific verification settings.
  • Prevent repeated or unauthorized verification activity.
  • Respond to privacy requests and manage user preferences.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the bot.

3. Verification Codes

Verifier may generate temporary verification codes when a user begins verification. Codes are intended to be used only for the verification process and expire after the configured expiration period.

Verification codes are stored in hashed form where applicable. Temporary verification information is used to validate submitted codes and manage verification sessions.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in a local SQLite database or another storage system used to operate Verifier.

Temporary verification information is retained only as needed to complete or manage verification sessions. Other records may remain stored while necessary to provide the bot's functionality, maintain server settings, or honor privacy preferences.

Some privacy preferences may be retained after other information is deleted so that previously selected privacy choices continue to be respected.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed through Discord and its services as necessary for the bot to function. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service.

6. Discord

Verifier operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy. We do not control how Discord independently collects or processes information.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

7. Your Privacy Choices

Verifier provides privacy-related commands where available, including the ability to view stored information, request deletion, export information, and opt out of certain data collection or processing.

Some information may need to be retained for security, operational, legal, or preference-management purposes.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Verifier is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that information has been collected inappropriately, contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the bot's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published with a revised effective date.
